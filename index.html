03 · Desktop app

Cipher: Encrypted File Vault

A private vault for documents and photos. Files are encrypted on the device before they are written to disk, so nothing stored in the vault can be read without the owner's password.

  • AES-256-GCM encryption with keys derived from the password using scrypt
  • Recovery key, two-factor login and one-time backup codes
  • Encrypted local backups and optional Google Drive backup
  • Starred files, tags, notes, trash with 30-day recovery and an activity log
  • Upload scanning that checks file types and flags risky PDFs
  • Auto-lock, screenshot protection, and ring, list and grid views

Community project · Team lead

Digital Bridge.

I led a team teaching digital skills to people who were left behind by technology. We researched the problem, surveyed the community, ran workshops, and measured the result.

Research

  • Causes, consequences, and global, national and local perspectives from recognised sources
  • 74 in-person surveys plus an online survey with about 200 responses
  • Interviews with a Systems International development head, a UVAS IT head and a software engineer

Action

  • Poster campaign and door-to-door outreach to shops and neighbours
  • 2 hands-on workshops covering WhatsApp, phishing and mobile use
  • Surveys at Education University, a public park, UVAS, and among neighbours and family
  • Pre and post surveys plus participant feedback

Measured impact

Share of participants able to do each skill, before and after the workshops.

Participant feedback: 73% found it very helpful, 80% enjoyed it very much, 80% felt very comfortable.

Writing

Research report.

“Do human errors contribute to the rise of cybersecurity threats?”

A report examining weak passwords, phishing and misconfigured systems as causes of breaches. It covers the financial and reputational consequences, weighs different perspectives, and argues that human ignorance is the most significant cause.
